## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 890 ms for the official emulator and 2.14 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1529 ms for the official emulator and 8.60 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 272 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.29 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 67.2 ms for the official emulator and 10.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.86 s for the official emulator and 1617 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5599 ms for the official emulator and 132 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6714 ms for the official emulator and 1837 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 185.57 s for the official emulator and 2330 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 173 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 18.4 ms for the official emulator and 11.7 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1989 ms for the official emulator and 1707 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 121.27 s for the official emulator and 1603 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 116 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1756 ms for the official emulator and 868 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2036 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
