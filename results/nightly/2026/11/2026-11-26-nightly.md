## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 951 ms for the official emulator and 2.20 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1638 ms for the official emulator and 9.18 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 284 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.54 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 69.8 ms for the official emulator and 11.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.71 s for the official emulator and 1582 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5920 ms for the official emulator and 136 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6989 ms for the official emulator and 1887 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 200.01 s for the official emulator and 2280 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 196 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 16.4 ms for the official emulator and 11.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1940 ms for the official emulator and 1674 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 121.01 s for the official emulator and 1601 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 110 ms for the official emulator and 0.03 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1839 ms for the official emulator and 935 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2039 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
