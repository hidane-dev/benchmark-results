## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 930 ms for the official emulator and 2.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1612 ms for the official emulator and 9.19 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 271 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.52 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 68.5 ms for the official emulator and 10.8 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.84 s for the official emulator and 1593 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5871 ms for the official emulator and 140 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 7053 ms for the official emulator and 1899 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 186.55 s for the official emulator and 2226 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 181 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1867 ms for the official emulator and 927 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2038 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
