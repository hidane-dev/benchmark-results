## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 817 ms for the official emulator and 1.50 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1419 ms for the official emulator and 5.47 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 8.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 281 MiB for the official emulator and 11.2 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.12 ms for the official emulator and 0.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 62.7 ms for the official emulator and 7.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 14.78 s for the official emulator and 1284 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5007 ms for the official emulator and 114 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6002 ms for the official emulator and 1512 ms for hidane.

Medians of the measured runs; every statistic and sample is in the JSON result.
