## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 874 ms for the official emulator and 2.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1526 ms for the official emulator and 8.51 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 276 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.65 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 65.7 ms for the official emulator and 10.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.02 s for the official emulator and 1495 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5413 ms for the official emulator and 130 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6678 ms for the official emulator and 1823 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 177.85 s for the official emulator and 2114 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 172 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1683 ms for the official emulator and 1594 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1767 ms for the official emulator and 892 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2040 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
