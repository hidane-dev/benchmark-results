## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 860 ms for the official emulator and 2.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1509 ms for the official emulator and 8.44 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 271 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.41 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 61.8 ms for the official emulator and 10.2 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.40 s for the official emulator and 1476 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5460 ms for the official emulator and 129 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6516 ms for the official emulator and 1791 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 163.00 s for the official emulator and 2078 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 158 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 15.1 ms for the official emulator and 10.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1682 ms for the official emulator and 1536 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 106.69 s for the official emulator and 1467 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 101 ms for the official emulator and 0.02 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1711 ms for the official emulator and 864 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2028 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
