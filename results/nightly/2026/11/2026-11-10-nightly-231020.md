## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 871 ms for the official emulator and 2.14 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1513 ms for the official emulator and 8.59 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 270 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.23 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 67.3 ms for the official emulator and 10.7 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.17 s for the official emulator and 1620 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5654 ms for the official emulator and 135 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6749 ms for the official emulator and 1841 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 190.43 s for the official emulator and 2312 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 187 ms for the official emulator and 0.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 15.5 ms for the official emulator and 11.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1800 ms for the official emulator and 1772 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 125.02 s for the official emulator and 1539 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 118 ms for the official emulator and 0.02 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1752 ms for the official emulator and 874 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2036 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
