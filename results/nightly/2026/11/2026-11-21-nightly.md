## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 845 ms for the official emulator and 2.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1467 ms for the official emulator and 8.30 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 279 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.29 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 60.7 ms for the official emulator and 10.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.68 s for the official emulator and 1431 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5199 ms for the official emulator and 126 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6394 ms for the official emulator and 1759 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 159.05 s for the official emulator and 2040 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 160 ms for the official emulator and 0.06 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 15.1 ms for the official emulator and 10.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1707 ms for the official emulator and 1501 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 104.60 s for the official emulator and 1444 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 97.0 ms for the official emulator and 0.02 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1676 ms for the official emulator and 843 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2031 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
