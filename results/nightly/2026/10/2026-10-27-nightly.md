## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 823 ms for the official emulator and 2.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1430 ms for the official emulator and 7.45 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 271 MiB for the official emulator and 16.0 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.19 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 61.7 ms for the official emulator and 9.91 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.17 s for the official emulator and 1415 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5095 ms for the official emulator and 122 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6251 ms for the official emulator and 1744 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 162.30 s for the official emulator and 2241 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 163 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 13.5 ms for the official emulator and 10.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1731 ms for the official emulator and 1456 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 93.99 s for the official emulator and 1284 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1635 ms for the official emulator and 819 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2024 MiB for the official emulator and 303 MiB for hidane.
