## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 868 ms for the official emulator and 2.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1498 ms for the official emulator and 7.68 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.4 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 268 MiB for the official emulator and 15.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.26 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 66.4 ms for the official emulator and 10.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.95 s for the official emulator and 1498 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5434 ms for the official emulator and 131 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6412 ms for the official emulator and 1796 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 170.78 s for the official emulator and 2339 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 171 ms for the official emulator and 0.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 14.4 ms for the official emulator and 10.7 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1748 ms for the official emulator and 1564 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 110.90 s for the official emulator and 1360 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 104 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1731 ms for the official emulator and 873 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2045 MiB for the official emulator and 303 MiB for hidane.
