## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 924 ms for the official emulator and 2.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1612 ms for the official emulator and 8.27 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 273 MiB for the official emulator and 16.0 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.27 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 66.4 ms for the official emulator and 11.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.27 s for the official emulator and 1541 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5596 ms for the official emulator and 135 ms for hidane.
