## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 841 ms for the official emulator and 2.13 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1466 ms for the official emulator and 8.46 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 270 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.19 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 60.9 ms for the official emulator and 9.89 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 14.49 s for the official emulator and 1419 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5174 ms for the official emulator and 126 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6197 ms for the official emulator and 1751 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 154.34 s for the official emulator and 2008 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 153 ms for the official emulator and 0.05 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 14.1 ms for the official emulator and 9.97 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1634 ms for the official emulator and 1493 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 75.6 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1669 ms for the official emulator and 839 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2029 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
