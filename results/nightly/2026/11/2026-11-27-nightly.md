- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 861 ms for the official emulator and 2.14 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 271 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.27 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 63.2 ms for the official emulator and 10.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 14.93 s for the official emulator and 1448 ms for hidane.
