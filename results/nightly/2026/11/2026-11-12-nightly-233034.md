- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 919 ms for the official emulator and 2.11 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 267 MiB for the official emulator and 12.6 MiB for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 63.6 ms for the official emulator and 10.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.60 s for the official emulator and 1508 ms for hidane.
