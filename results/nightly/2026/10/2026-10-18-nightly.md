- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 876 ms for the official emulator and 2.11 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1522 ms for the official emulator and 7.75 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 276 MiB for the official emulator and 16.1 MiB for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 64.3 ms for the official emulator and 10.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.60 s for the official emulator and 1480 ms for hidane.
