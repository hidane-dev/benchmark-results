## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 887 ms for the official emulator and 1.50 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1541 ms for the official emulator and 5.75 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 274 MiB for the official emulator and 11.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.31 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 67.0 ms for the official emulator and 99.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.96 s for the official emulator and 19.12 s for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5457 ms for the official emulator and 128 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6582 ms for the official emulator and 1614 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 183.09 s for the official emulator and 19.73 s for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 178 ms for the official emulator and 0.24 ms for hidane.

Medians of the measured runs; every statistic and sample is in the JSON result.
