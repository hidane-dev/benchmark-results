## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 859 ms for the official emulator and 1.52 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1488 ms for the official emulator and 5.78 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.0 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 266 MiB for the official emulator and 11.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.24 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 63.9 ms for the official emulator and 10.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.78 s for the official emulator and 1424 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5288 ms for the official emulator and 123 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6494 ms for the official emulator and 1763 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 167.98 s for the official emulator and 2321 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 175 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.

Medians of the measured runs; every statistic and sample is in the JSON result.
