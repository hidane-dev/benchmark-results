## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 852 ms for the official emulator and 1.53 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1478 ms for the official emulator and 5.55 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 276 MiB for the official emulator and 11.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.59 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 65.4 ms for the official emulator and 10.2 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.60 s for the official emulator and 1540 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5311 ms for the official emulator and 121 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6466 ms for the official emulator and 1558 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 175.64 s for the official emulator and 2436 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 174 ms for the official emulator and 0.13 ms for hidane.

Medians of the measured runs; every statistic and sample is in the JSON result.
