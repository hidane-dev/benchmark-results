## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 872 ms for the official emulator and 2.11 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1524 ms for the official emulator and 8.54 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 273 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.35 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 64.6 ms for the official emulator and 10.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.70 s for the official emulator and 1500 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5371 ms for the official emulator and 128 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6519 ms for the official emulator and 1810 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 167.75 s for the official emulator and 2106 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 165 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 15.4 ms for the official emulator and 10.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1734 ms for the official emulator and 1560 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 112.48 s for the official emulator and 1489 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 106 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1731 ms for the official emulator and 887 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2044 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
