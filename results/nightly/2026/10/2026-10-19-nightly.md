## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 925 ms for the official emulator and 2.14 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1601 ms for the official emulator and 8.26 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 273 MiB for the official emulator and 16.1 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.33 ms for the official emulator and 0.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 68.9 ms for the official emulator and 11.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.94 s for the official emulator and 1608 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5565 ms for the official emulator and 135 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6466 ms for the official emulator and 1799 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 162.68 s for the official emulator and 2405 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 168 ms for the official emulator and 0.11 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1744 ms for the official emulator and 1552 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2038 MiB for the official emulator and 303 MiB for hidane.
