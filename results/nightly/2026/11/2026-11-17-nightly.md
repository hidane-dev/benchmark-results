## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 898 ms for the official emulator and 2.13 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1554 ms for the official emulator and 8.68 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 275 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.42 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 67.5 ms for the official emulator and 10.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.33 s for the official emulator and 1509 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5497 ms for the official emulator and 131 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6640 ms for the official emulator and 1819 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 176.58 s for the official emulator and 2162 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 171 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 14.3 ms for the official emulator and 10.6 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1861 ms for the official emulator and 1584 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 107.99 s for the official emulator and 1536 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 101 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1779 ms for the official emulator and 893 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2037 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
