## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 941 ms for the official emulator and 1.56 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1617 ms for the official emulator and 6.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 7.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 268 MiB for the official emulator and 10.1 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.42 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 69.2 ms for the official emulator and 8.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 17.14 s for the official emulator and 1461 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5643 ms for the official emulator and 126 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6751 ms for the official emulator and 1673 ms for hidane.

Medians of the measured runs; every statistic and sample is in the JSON result.
