- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 272 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 62.4 ms for the official emulator and 9.84 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.05 s for the official emulator and 1416 ms for hidane.
