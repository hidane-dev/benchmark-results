## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 862 ms for the official emulator and 2.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1506 ms for the official emulator and 8.44 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 278 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.27 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 61.3 ms for the official emulator and 9.98 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.61 s for the official emulator and 1423 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5580 ms for the official emulator and 131 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6679 ms for the official emulator and 1806 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 186.81 s for the official emulator and 2103 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 180 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1797 ms for the official emulator and 1539 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1725 ms for the official emulator and 867 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2039 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
