## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 836 ms for the official emulator and 2.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1446 ms for the official emulator and 7.66 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 274 MiB for the official emulator and 16.0 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.40 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 64.0 ms for the official emulator and 10.1 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.13 s for the official emulator and 1440 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5200 ms for the official emulator and 125 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6282 ms for the official emulator and 1776 ms for hidane.
