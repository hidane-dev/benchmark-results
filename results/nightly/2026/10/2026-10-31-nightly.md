## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 883 ms for the official emulator and 2.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1541 ms for the official emulator and 8.62 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 270 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.27 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 66.2 ms for the official emulator and 10.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.84 s for the official emulator and 1499 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5387 ms for the official emulator and 128 ms for hidane.
