## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 942 ms for the official emulator and 2.11 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1618 ms for the official emulator and 8.34 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 275 MiB for the official emulator and 16.1 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.67 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 68.3 ms for the official emulator and 11.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.91 s for the official emulator and 1600 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5570 ms for the official emulator and 135 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 165 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2032 MiB for the official emulator and 303 MiB for hidane.
