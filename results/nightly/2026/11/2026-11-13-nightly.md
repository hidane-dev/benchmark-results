## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 875 ms for the official emulator and 2.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1523 ms for the official emulator and 8.61 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 147 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 272 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.37 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 64.3 ms for the official emulator and 10.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.53 s for the official emulator and 1431 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5392 ms for the official emulator and 126 ms for hidane.
