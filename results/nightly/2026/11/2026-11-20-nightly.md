## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 951 ms for the official emulator and 2.16 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1662 ms for the official emulator and 9.19 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 266 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.60 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 70.2 ms for the official emulator and 11.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 17.18 s for the official emulator and 1590 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5791 ms for the official emulator and 138 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6900 ms for the official emulator and 1901 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 196.24 s for the official emulator and 2277 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 191 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 14.3 ms for the official emulator and 11.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1867 ms for the official emulator and 1681 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 128.80 s for the official emulator and 1622 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 120 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1899 ms for the official emulator and 949 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2043 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
