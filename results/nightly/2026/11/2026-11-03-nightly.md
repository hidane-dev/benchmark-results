## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 894 ms for the official emulator and 2.12 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1532 ms for the official emulator and 8.95 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 147 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 277 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.38 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 66.4 ms for the official emulator and 10.8 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 17.13 s for the official emulator and 1599 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5606 ms for the official emulator and 134 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6552 ms for the official emulator and 1820 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 169.91 s for the official emulator and 2188 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2040 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
