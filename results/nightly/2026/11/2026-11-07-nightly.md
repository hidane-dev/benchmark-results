## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 983 ms for the official emulator and 2.19 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1708 ms for the official emulator and 9.04 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.8 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 288 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.55 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 69.3 ms for the official emulator and 11.2 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.94 s for the official emulator and 1570 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 6413 ms for the official emulator and 154 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 7487 ms for the official emulator and 1912 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 221.14 s for the official emulator and 2218 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 209 ms for the official emulator and 0.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 17.8 ms for the official emulator and 11.3 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1974 ms for the official emulator and 1662 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 156.55 s for the official emulator and 1584 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 149 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1890 ms for the official emulator and 946 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2032 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
