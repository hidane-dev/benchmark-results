## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 851 ms for the official emulator and 2.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1483 ms for the official emulator and 8.33 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.9 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 272 MiB for the official emulator and 13.0 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.32 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 65.1 ms for the official emulator and 10.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.93 s for the official emulator and 1562 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5490 ms for the official emulator and 129 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6525 ms for the official emulator and 1790 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 175.15 s for the official emulator and 2220 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 169 ms for the official emulator and 0.11 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 16.2 ms for the official emulator and 10.7 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1766 ms for the official emulator and 1721 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 124.90 s for the official emulator and 1511 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 121 ms for the official emulator and 0.02 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1798 ms for the official emulator and 903 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2038 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
