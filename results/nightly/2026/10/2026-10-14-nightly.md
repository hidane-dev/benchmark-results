- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 892 ms for the official emulator and 1.55 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 273 MiB for the official emulator and 11.9 MiB for hidane.
