## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 950 ms for the official emulator and 2.11 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1641 ms for the official emulator and 9.28 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.6 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 273 MiB for the official emulator and 12.7 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.52 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 70.3 ms for the official emulator and 11.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 17.03 s for the official emulator and 1619 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5805 ms for the official emulator and 139 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6862 ms for the official emulator and 1902 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 184 ms for the official emulator and 0.07 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1854 ms for the official emulator and 1698 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2039 MiB for the official emulator and 300 MiB for hidane.
