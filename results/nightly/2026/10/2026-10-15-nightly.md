## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 873 ms for the official emulator and 2.13 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1515 ms for the official emulator and 6.24 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 11.2 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 279 MiB for the official emulator and 14.2 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.35 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 62.5 ms for the official emulator and 10.2 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.00 s for the official emulator and 1452 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 178.08 s for the official emulator and 2250 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 117 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
