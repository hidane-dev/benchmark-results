- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 947 ms for the official emulator and 2.14 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1643 ms for the official emulator and 8.35 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 271 MiB for the official emulator and 16.0 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.38 ms for the official emulator and 0.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 70.8 ms for the official emulator and 11.6 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.99 s for the official emulator and 1630 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5875 ms for the official emulator and 139 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6803 ms for the official emulator and 1900 ms for hidane.
