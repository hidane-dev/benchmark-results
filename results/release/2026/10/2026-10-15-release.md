## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 981 ms for the official emulator and 1.56 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1684 ms for the official emulator and 6.30 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 9.4 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 267 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.78 ms for the official emulator and 0.10 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 72.2 ms for the official emulator and 11.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 17.54 s for the official emulator and 1610 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5773 ms for the official emulator and 135 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6811 ms for the official emulator and 1691 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 191.21 s for the official emulator and 2511 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 195 ms for the official emulator and 0.15 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 116 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
