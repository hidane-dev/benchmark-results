- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 274 MiB for the official emulator and 12.9 MiB for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 62.7 ms for the official emulator and 10.0 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 15.44 s for the official emulator and 1446 ms for hidane.
