## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 944 ms for the official emulator and 2.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1627 ms for the official emulator and 8.28 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 273 MiB for the official emulator and 16.1 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.43 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 70.7 ms for the official emulator and 11.5 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 17.14 s for the official emulator and 1611 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5750 ms for the official emulator and 138 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6721 ms for the official emulator and 1893 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 186.45 s for the official emulator and 2538 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 185 ms for the official emulator and 0.08 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit with the owner credential, as the Admin SDKs send it, was 17.1 ms for the official emulator and 11.9 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1959 ms for the official emulator and 1699 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection was 115.73 s for the official emulator and 1500 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a `limit(50)` listener on the collection, was 110 ms for the official emulator and 0.01 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1871 ms for the official emulator and 948 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2041 MiB for the official emulator and 303 MiB for hidane.
