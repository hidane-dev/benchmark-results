## Summary

- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the port accepted connections was 884 ms for the official emulator and 2.13 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time from process start until the first gRPC request was answered was 1545 ms for the official emulator and 8.04 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory when idle was 148 MiB for the official emulator and 12.3 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median resident memory after writing 1,000 documents was 268 MiB for the official emulator and 16.1 MiB for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a single-document read over gRPC was 2.39 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median latency of a 500-write batch commit was 70.9 ms for the official emulator and 11.4 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 was 16.95 s for the official emulator and 1592 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of the synthetic CI workload (start, ready, seed, 200 tests, shutdown) was 5638 ms for the official emulator and 133 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median wall-clock time of `firebase emulators:exec` around the same workload (200 tests) was 6717 ms for the official emulator and 1839 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection was 169.28 s for the official emulator and 2515 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median growth of a commit's time per 10,000 documents already stored, writing 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with a listener on the collection, was 168 ms for the official emulator and 0.09 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time to write 100,000 documents in commits of 500 with the owner credential was 1668 ms for the official emulator and 1574 ms for hidane.

- In this benchmark environment, median time from `firebase emulators:start --only firestore` until the Firestore port accepted a connection was 1879 ms for the official emulator and 927 ms for hidane.
- In this benchmark environment, median RSS after writing 500,000 documents was 2040 MiB for the official emulator and 303 MiB for hidane.
